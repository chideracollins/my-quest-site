What stays on your device

Your journal entries, including titles and text, are stored only on your device using a local database. They are not uploaded to our servers.

The app also keeps small amounts of data on your device to remember your settings and cache app data. This stays on your device too.

You can delete individual journal entries from within the app. Deleting an entry removes it from your device's local storage.

Account and sign-in information

My Quest uses Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to create and manage your account. When you sign up, your name, email address and password are processed by Firebase Authentication so you can log in across devices and app updates.

We do not see or store your password. You may also be asked to verify your email address; verification emails are sent on your behalf.

If you sign out, your account remains with Firebase Authentication, but your journal entries stay on your device.

Journal entries and AI processing

The AI features that generate writing prompts and suggested journal titles run on your device. Your journal text is processed by a language model stored locally on your phone, not by a server we control.

Because the AI runs locally, your journal writing is not sent off-device for AI processing.

AI model downloads

Before you can use the AI features, the app downloads an AI model to your device from Hugging Face, a third-party model hosting service. This download is initiated by you and does not include your journal content.

When downloading, your device communicates directly with Hugging Face. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to that interaction.

Diagnostics and crash reporting

The app uses Sentry, a third-party error-tracking service, to detect crashes and errors so we can fix them. Error reports may include technical information such as the app version, device type, operating system version, and a description of the error, along with performance traces.

We have disabled features that would capture your screen or attach personal information to error reports. We do not intend for the content of your journal entries to be included in these reports.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The app shares information with the following third parties only as needed to operate: Google (Firebase Authentication) for account management, Hugging Face for AI model downloads, and Sentry for crash and error reporting.

Account sign-in and communication with these services happen over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Retention and deletion

Journal entries remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored journal data.

Your account information is held by Firebase Authentication until the account is deleted. You can request deletion of your account and its associated data by contacting us; we will delete it rather than merely deactivate it.

We may retain minimal data where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, and we will disclose any such retention here.

App security

The app offers an optional PIN lock screen. When enabled, the app requires the PIN before it can be used.

Your password is never stored on your device by the app; sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Children's privacy

My Quest is rated for ages 13 and up. Depending on your country, you may be considered a child under local law, and rules like COPPA (US) and the GDPR (EU) may require parental consent before an account can be created or personal data processed.

Because journal entries stay on your device, we do not receive your journal content. However, creating an account does share your email address with Firebase Authentication, and error reports may be shared with Sentry as described above.

We do not knowingly allow children below the minimum digital consent age in their country to create accounts without the consent their local law requires. If you believe a child has created an account without required consent, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be published in the app with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, or about how your information is handled, please contact us. You can also review our developer identity in the app's Google Play store listing.
