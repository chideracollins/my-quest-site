My Quest — Account Deletion

Delete your account

This page explains how to request deletion of your My Quest account and the data associated with it. My Quest is a privacy-focused journaling app by Quantum Intelligence Lab.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap or click the button below. It opens an email to our support address with the subject and details pre-filled.
  2. Send the email from the address you use to sign in to My Quest — this is how we verify the request is yours.
  3. We process verified deletion requests and confirm by reply email once your account has been deleted.
Request account deletion by email

If the button doesn't work, email qintel247@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my My Quest account".

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data type What happens
Your account (name, email, password) Deleted. Removed from Firebase Authentication when your request is processed.
Journal entries Never stored by us. Journal entries live only on your device. Delete them in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
Crash and error reports (Sentry) Existing reports may be retained by Sentry for a limited period under Sentry's own retention policy (typically 90 days). These reports do not contain your journal content.
Verification: we can only delete an account when the request comes from the account's own email address. If you no longer have access to it, tell us in your email and we'll confirm another way to verify your identity.
See also our Privacy Policy. This deletion process reflects how the app currently works and does not constitute legal advice.
